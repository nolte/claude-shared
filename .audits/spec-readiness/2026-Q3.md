## Resolutions landed in this frame

### Load-bearing Open Questions from #117

- **OQ-3 — Auto-generated catalog pages: generator-fixed track vs per-author** → **RESOLVED**
  - Decision: **generator-fixed**. Catalog pages produced by `spec/claude/skill-agent-catalog/` carry `track: developer-docs` from the generator, not from per-skill / per-agent author declaration.
  - Spec landed in `spec/claude/skill-agent-catalog/` (en + de) §Generation mechanism as a MUST clause plus a matching AC, per the issue's "preferred" target.
  - Cross-reference in `spec/project/docs-audience-tracks/` (en + de) §Per-page contract via the meta-pages bullet (see OQ-5 below).
- **OQ-5 — Meta-pages: `developer-docs` fallback vs new `meta` track value** → **RESOLVED**
  - Decision: **`developer-docs` as portfolio default**, no new `meta` track value. Meta pages are exempt from the audience-to-track no-contradiction rule (§Audience-to-track mapping) per the existing §Content-mode mapping, since `content_mode: meta` already signals that the page routes readers across tracks.
  - Spec landed in `spec/project/docs-audience-tracks/` (en + de) §Per-page contract as a SHOULD clause covering Home pages, per-section index pages, literate-nav `SUMMARY.md`, tag indexes, and ADRs that motivate the track split itself.
