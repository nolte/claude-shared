- **Phase 6c (audience↔track consistency):** correctly surfaces `docs/{en,de}/getting-started/usage.md` as an `Audience-track mismatch` warning (`audience: [maintainer, external-contributor]` → developer-docs, but `track: user-docs`), and correctly emits nothing for `content_mode: meta` pages (exemption holds). No false positives. **Validated.**
