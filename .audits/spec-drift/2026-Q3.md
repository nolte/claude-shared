# Spec Drift Audit — 2026-Q3 (full scope)

## Scope

**Trigger:** quarterly full-scope audit per `spec/project/spec-drift-audit/` §Triggers and cadence, run as the supersession of the 2026-Q2 tracker (issue #453, whose closing criterion reads "Close by re-running `spec-drift-audit` for the next quarter; the fresh report supersedes this list"). This artifact is that fresh report and takes over the Q2 record's map function (see §Q2-to-Q3 carry-over).

**In-scope specs:** all **105** topics under `spec/<topic>/<slug>/en.md`. Every one carries a non-empty `## Requirements` or `## Acceptance Criteria` section, so nothing was excluded on that basis. `Status: draft` specs are in scope per the governing spec; 98 of the 105 are draft, 7 are accepted. Canonical language `en` per `spec/.spec-config.yml`.

This closes the Q2 finding `project/spec-drift-audit.ac-coverage-gap`, which recorded that the Q2 artifact covered 41 specs against a then-current corpus of 62 and demanded the Q3 artifact either cover the full corpus or list its omissions. **No spec was omitted.**

**Excluded specs:** none. `spec/schemas/` holds JSON Schema files, not spec topics, and is out of the perimeter by construction (it is itself audited *as* an implementation surface under `project/yaml-json-schema`).

**Implementation surfaces examined:** `skills/`, `agents/`, `plugins/{nolte-media,nolte-engineering,nolte-claude-dev}/{skills,agents}/`, `.claude-plugin/`, `.github/` (workflows, `settings.yml`, `release-automation.yml`, ISSUE_TEMPLATE, PR template), `.claude/` (`settings.json`, `rules/`), `Taskfile.yml`, `mkdocs.yml`, `docs/{en,de}/`, `scripts/`, `tests/`, `project/`, `portfolio/`, `design/`, `.pre-commit-config.yaml`, `.vale.ini`, `renovate.json5`, `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `AUDIENCES.md`, `.audits/`, plus live GitHub state via `gh`.

**Method:** each spec's normative clauses (MUST / MUST NOT / SHOULD / SHOULD NOT / MAY) and each acceptance-criterion item were scored `pass` / `fail` / `blocked` / `not-applicable` against the actual repository state, by eleven read-only batch auditors working disjoint slices of the corpus, then deduplicated and triaged in the main session. Every finding below carries file:line evidence; findings that couldn't be verified against a file or command output were scored `blocked`, never guessed `pass`.

## Summary

**Overall posture: markedly improved, and the improvement is real rather than an artifact of narrower scope.** Q2 audited 62 specs and produced 220 findings (66 Critical). Q3 audited 105 specs — 69 % more surface — and produced **44** findings (25 Critical). Per-criterion, 1974 of 2331 evaluated criteria pass; **75 of 105 specs have zero failing criteria.** The 2026-07-24/25 clean-up wave that closed #457, #460, #461, #464, #469, #470, and #471 is the direct cause, and this audit confirms it held.

The highest-severity finding is not a single defect but a **pattern**: the 2026-07-24 settlement wave answered load-bearing Open Questions inside the test-cycle and design specs, and in several cases the *spec* moved while the *artifact operationalising it* did not. Four Critical findings in the test-cycle family (#491) and four in the brand/design family (#494) share that shape. The second pattern is **declared-but-never-run cadence**: `dependency-audit` and `license-check` both mandate a recurring gated check that this repository has never performed (#496), and `skill-review` has covered 8 of 63 skills (#492).

**Go/no-go:** NO-GO for a clean bill of health — 24 of the 25 Critical findings are open (F4 is fixed here). **GO** on the audit's own contract: every one of the 44 findings carries a documented decision per `spec/project/spec-drift-audit/` §Feedback loop, 9 are fixed in this PR, and the remaining 35 are tracked on eight labelled follow-up issues with named specialists. No finding is deferred to an unbounded future, and none sits without a decision.
